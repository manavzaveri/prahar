# PRAHAR: four-minute demonstration

Use the desktop view for the recording. All example factories, jobs and tariffs
are synthetic. Do not describe these numbers as measured plant results.

1. **0:00–0:35 — Problem and boundaries.** Five factories may each select the
   same cheap hours. PRAHAR checks whether the combined load fits their shared
   supply limit. It is an advisory simulation; the supervisor stays in control.
2. **0:35–1:30 — Compare the three schedules.** Start with Shared headroom. The
   original schedule costs INR 12,500 in energy charges; independent scheduling
   costs INR 11,300 but exceeds the 140 kW shared limit. Coordination also costs
   INR 11,300 and fits the limit. Peak is 200/200/140 kW and consumption stays
   1,500 kWh. The INR 1,200 saving comes from time shifting; coordination adds
   feasibility in this example, with no additional cash saving.
3. **1:30–2:10 — Show human control.** Click Keep original time for the first
   activity. Its original 18:00 start is retained. The result recalculates;
   retaining this activity raises coordinated energy charges to INR 11,540.
4. **2:10–2:50 — Stress the idea.** Select Tighter supply. Coordination costs
   INR 240 more than independent scheduling to fit the 90 kW limit. Then select
   No movable activities: the app reports infeasibility and leaves unavailable
   results blank. Neither outcome is hidden.
5. **2:50–3:35 — Show what is actually built.** Open Inputs & passports. Show
   windows, duration, resource, power, factory capacities and prices. Explain
   that CSV/JSON imports are processed in the browser. Open Evidence & scope
   to show test coverage and the limits of the claim.
6. **3:35–4:00 — Close with evidence and next validation.** Reset to Shared
   headroom, export the readable report and show its complete inputs. Propose
   validating one auxiliary activity with a consenting plant supervisor before
   considering an inter-factory pilot. Demand charges, carbon credits, payments
   from a utility, automatic plant control and measured production outcomes are
   outside the present prototype.

The demonstration must be in your own words. Do not claim organizer permission
for AI-assisted code has been confirmed; clarify that separately before the
competition submission.
